# Interactive Multi-Country Election Simulator

An interactive, client-side election simulator featuring parliament hemicycles, interactive maps, coalition builders, and country-specific electoral systems:

- **🇭🇺 Hungary (1990 – Sept 2026):** All parliamentary elections from 1990 to 2026 + Sept 2026 scenario, with party leaders, county constituency grids (176 SMCs in 1990–2010; 106 SMCs in 2014–2026), fragment-vote & winner-surplus compensation, and D'Hondt list allocation.
- **🏙️ Budapest (2024 Mayoral & General Assembly):** Interactive 23-district map (`I. Budavár` – `XXIII. Soroksár`), Mayoral race (Gergely Karácsony vs. Dávid Vitézy) and 33-seat General Assembly (TISZA, FIDESZ–KDNP, DK–MSZP–Párbeszéd, MKKP, Vitézy–LMP).
- **🇪🇺 European Union (2024 European Parliament):** 720 MEPs across 27 member states and 9 political groups.
- **🇩🇪 Germany (2025 Bundestag):** 630 seats (Sainte-Laguë, 5% threshold) across all 16 *Bundesländer*.
- **🇫🇷 France (2027 Presidential & Legislative):** Two-round presidential runoff with editable vote-transfer matrix and 577-seat *Assemblée nationale* two-round constituency simulator.
- **🇬🇧 United Kingdom (2024 General Election):** 650-constituency hex cartogram (FPTP) with official 2024 constituency results, uniform & proportional swing, and drag-to-paint overrides.
- **🇺🇸 United States (2028 Presidential):** 538-vote Electoral College map (including Maine & Nebraska congressional districts) and customizable party tickets.
